Become a Guest

We're looking for leaders with stories that matter. If you fit the criteria below, we'd love to hear from you.

Transglobal Relevance

Your story, expertise, or brand has international reach and resonates across borders. We're looking for guests who bring perspectives that transcend local markets.

Industry Relevance

You work in merchandising, promotional products, branding, advertising, or the global marketing industry. Your insights contribute to the evolution of how brands connect with people.

English Proficiency

You can communicate fluently in English as a second language. Our conversations are in English to reach a global audience.

A Story Worth Telling

You have a compelling narrative—whether it's building a brand from scratch, navigating cultural challenges, pioneering sustainability, or driving meaningful change in your industry.
